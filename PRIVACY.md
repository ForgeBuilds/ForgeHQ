# Privacy Policy (Summary)

The full Privacy Policy & Terms of Use are available in-app at
`/privacypolicyandterms` and linked from the website footer.

## Key points

* Struct is a local-first desktop app. Local features run on your device.
* Account, cloud sync, and backend-routed AI requests use the Forge backend
  and may involve third-party providers (e.g. OpenRouter).
* We collect account data (email, auth records, preferences) to provide the
  service.
* Data may be used for training, evaluation, security, and service improvement.
* You can delete local data and request account deletion.
* Struct is free — no payments are processed.

For the complete policy, see `components/Privacypolicyandterms.tsx`.
